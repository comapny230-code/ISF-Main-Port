# Venue and travel information record

This record documents the facts used on the Venue & Travel page. Dynamic schedules, fares, hotel availability, pickup services, and journey times are deliberately excluded because they can change and have not been confirmed for the event.

## Confirmed venue

- [GIET University contact page](https://www.giet.edu/contact/) — official campus address: Gobriguda, Po-Kharling, Gunupur, Rayagada, Odisha 765022; university telephone details.
- [GIET University mandatory disclosure](https://www.giet.edu/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/MANDATORY-DISCLOSURE-27-6-20-1.pdf) — identifies Gunupur as the nearest railway station at approximately 5 km and Visakhapatnam as the nearest airport at approximately 210 km. These figures are presented as approximate planning distances.

## Airports and railway stations

- [Airports Authority of India: Visakhapatnam Airport](https://www.aai.aero/en/airports/contact-us/visakhapatnam) — confirms the operating airport name, location, and AAI contact information.
- [Airports Authority of India: Biju Patnaik International Airport](https://www.aai.aero/en/airports/bhubaneswar) — confirms Bhubaneswar airport as an alternative gateway.
- [Government of Odisha: Aviation](https://ct.odisha.gov.in/en/transport/aviation/About) — identifies Jeypore Airport in Koraput as an operating regional airport providing intra-state and inter-state flights. Because services are limited and can change, the page tells visitors to confirm the operating flight before relying on this option.
- [AAI airport-distance record for the Gunupur area](https://www.aai.aero/sites/default/files/DETAILS-UNSERVED-AS-ON22NOV16.pdf) and Government of India environmental records place Jeypore roughly 195–205 km from locations in the Gunupur area. The page therefore displays a range rather than a false point estimate.
- [East Coast Railway directory](https://eastcoastrail.indianrailways.gov.in/uploads/files/1721652967747-HQ%20DIRECTORY_%20FINAL.pdf) — confirms station names and codes for Gunupur (GNPR), Srikakulam Road (CHE), and Palasa (PSA).
- [East Coast Railway network map](https://eastcoastrail.indianrailways.gov.in/uploads/files/1654685338881-ECo%20Rail%20Map%20A3_22.pdf) — confirms Gunupur, Rayagada, Palasa, and the NH-326 regional corridor in the railway geography.
- [Indian Railways enquiry](https://enquiry.indianrail.gov.in/) — linked for live train-specific checking. The website does not state fixed train schedules.

## Event and accommodation data

- The event dates, venue, organizer contact, and local hotel telephone data come from the project's supplied event materials and assets.
- Hotel ratings, review counts, priority check-in, delegate tariffs, and event shuttle claims are not shown because their current status could not be confirmed from authoritative event information.
- Map actions pass the confirmed address or listed hotel address to Google Maps. They do not claim a fixed route or journey time.
